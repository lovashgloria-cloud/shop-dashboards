Shipping and returns

Is $5 shipping hurting? Partly. $5 is under Etsy's $6 threshold, so it is not a US search penalty (Etsy Seller Handbook: from Oct 1, 2024, US listings with shipping under $6 are prioritised). But it is not free: buyers do not see a "FREE shipping" badge, and the listings miss the free-shipping filter. Etsy says expensive shipping is a "top 10 barrier to purchase" and that moving shipping cost into the item price "can be an effective way to improve your sales" (Seller Handbook, "Creating Listings That Convert").

Recommendation: free shipping guarantee. Every active listing is $36.98 or more, so Etsy's free shipping guarantee (US orders/items $35+, Etsy Help "How to Offer Free Shipping") would make every US order ship free. Use Etsy's bulk tool to move the $5 into item prices, or absorb part of it on the leak listings being repriced anyway. Set the additional-item charge to $0 to encourage multi-item orders (4 of the 12-month orders were multi-unit).

International. The $5 / $5 USPS Priority Mail International rate looks far below what that service normally costs for a boxed ceramic or resin parcel. Check the real label cost. 5 countries were reached in 12 months, so a loss on each international order is plausible (not verified).

Processing and profile. Processing is "Made to order (3-5 days)" on all 37 listings. These are stocked, sourced items, so "Ready to ship" with true days is more accurate, and a review mentions delivery "after about a month". Etsy search rewards on-time shipping. Also rename the profile "uk" (it ships from US 94510) so it is not mistaken for a UK profile.

Returns. "No returns or exchanges" is a set policy, so there is no missing-policy penalty (Etsy Help: no policy at all ranks lower). But Etsy says "a clear return policy can give potential shoppers confidence" and that the return policy "is a factor of listing quality" in search (Seller Handbook, "How to Get 5-Star Reviews"). For $50-200 decor bought sight-unseen, with 3 size or quality complaints, "no returns" adds risk for the buyer. The shop already refunded or canceled 6 of 35 orders, so returns are effectively happening anyway. Recommend 14-30 day returns, buyer pays return postage, unused items. Fragile items: damage is still covered by the seller.

Can this be proven from the data? No. The data has no A/B test. The signals are 0.7% conversion despite 1,403 favorites, 5 abandoned carts in 12 months, and 28 coupon offers in 30 days with $0 redeemed, which together point to total price and risk. Make the shipping and returns change on a fixed date and compare 60-day conversion before and after.

Offsite Ads and fees

Rules. The shop has made far less than $10,000 in any 365 days (12-month revenue $2,484), so Offsite Ads cost 15% of an attributed order (capped at $100) and the shop can opt out (Etsy Help, "How Etsy's Offsite Ads Work").

What it cost. Statements show one Offsite Ads charge: PHP 805.34 in Aug 2026 (≈$12.83 at 62.77; equal to 15% of ≈$85.53 of attributed sales). In Aug, fees plus Offsite Ads were 20.5% of sales (standard fees 15.2% + Offsite 5.3%). In Sep, fees were 22.6% with no Offsite charge. Only Aug-Oct statements were captured (gap for earlier months).

Last 30 days. The Offsite Ads dashboard shows PHP 5,680.03 indirect revenue (≈$90.49, matching the Sep 15 dancer order of $90.50), 1 order and PHP 0.00 fees. That 1 order is the shop's only order in the last 30 days. A fee of ~$13.57 (15%) may still post; check the October statement (estimate).

Per-order impact. On the 12-month average order of $82.81, an Offsite-attributed sale costs ≈$12.42 extra on top of standard fees. Using the Aug statement ratio, an attributed order keeps roughly 80% of the sale before product and shipping cost.

Recommendation. Keep it ON through Q4. It costs nothing unless it sells, it drove the only recent order, and Google holiday traffic is high. Turn it OFF only if the product cost plus shipping leaves less than ~15% margin after standard fees (product costs are a gap). Re-check in January. Note also that the Etsy payment account runs in PHP with a 50% reserve held for 45 days (re-evaluated Nov 22) and a current balance of PHP -56.29. Plan cash for restocking the low-quantity sellers.

Coupons and sales cleanup

They granted 28 offers in 30 days (7 + 4 + 17) with $0 revenue. Whether those are still live is a gap.

Problems. 5% of a ~$70 item is ~$3.50, too small to move a buyer who hesitated at the price. Stale names ("MINE2025") look dated. Old 20-25% public codes, if still live, leak onto coupon sites and undercut full price. Discounts stacked on top of a permanently high price signal "the list price is not real".

Cleanup. Raise the favorited-item offer to 10-15% (MYFAVE fired 17 times in 30 days; the Zebra vase has 20 favorites and 0 orders) and the abandoned-cart offer to 10%. Rename the codes without years. End or verify SPRINGSALE26, AUGSALE2026, 25OFF and the 2025 bundled sale. Keep THANKYOU (repeat buyers: 0 in 12 months). Review pattern (observation only)

Four 5-star reviews from "Inactive account" buyers are dated Apr 11-12, 2025: three on #17 Zebra Stripe Ceramic Vase and one on #13 Mushroom Disco Ball. That is 4 of the 14 visible reviews, inside 2 days, early in the shop's life, all 5 stars, all from accounts that have since closed. This is a pattern only, not a conclusion: closed buyer accounts are common on Etsy. But Etsy's review-integrity rules treat incentivised or self-placed reviews as a violation, and this cluster is what a platform reviewer would look at first. Recommendation: do not rely on these reviews as social proof (for example in photo captions or the About text), and earn fresh reviews (packaging insert, a follow-up message through Etsy's own tools). Note also that the shop header shows 4.3 (16 reviews) while the Reviews page shows 4.5. Only 14 of the 16 reviews were visible.

Shop-level optimization

Catalog focus. The sales data changes the earlier "lead with vases, phase out party and novelty items" call. Revenue comes from three lines: Nordic wood figurines (Goose, Bear Family), dancer/abstract sculpture (Dancer) and retro disco decor (Mushroom ball, the No. 2 seller). Ceramic vases add the Morandi (4 sold) and Couple Heart Vase (1). The other 9 vases have 4-201 views and 0 orders, so the vase line needs better titles and photos before more vases are added. Off-niche items with 0 orders and low traffic (sailboat, apple) are review-in-90-days candidates, not immediate cuts. Gaps worth filling: travertine/stone (searches +83% to +132%), sculptural bookends (Bear Bookends proves demand), and a ballerina next to the Dancer.

Sections. Suggested sections: Vases · Abstract Sculptures · Nordic Wood Figurines · Shelf Styling (faux books, bookends) · Candle & Light · Gift Ideas (curated). Sections help shoppers browse and give Etsy category context.

About & announcement. The About blurb is one line ("Elegant interiors with a cozy, modern touch"). Suggested About (sample text; edit it to be true): "Home Glow Interiors is a small US-based decor shop run by Ashley. We curate Nordic, minimalist and abstract pieces: sculptural vases, abstract figures and shelf styling. Every listing shows real dimensions and materials, and we pack fragile pieces in fitted foam." Only say "US-based" or "ships from" if that is accurate; a review mentions delivery "after about a month". Announcement (last updated Oct 9, 2025): refresh it with the new sections, a size and material promise, current processing and delivery times, and a seasonal line (holiday gifting cut-off dates).

Photos. GAP: photos were not captured in the public scrape (no listing pages opened). Checks to run on each listing: 1) a plain or neutral first photo that matches across the shop; 2) one scale photo (hand, book or ruler); 3) a dimensions graphic; 4) a lifestyle shot on a shelf; 5) a material close-up, which is crucial for the faux books and resin pieces; 6) no supplier watermarks and no reused factory photos you lack rights to; 7) a 5-10 s video. Size and "cheap-looking" complaints suggest the current photos overstate scale and finish.

Pricing strategy. Proven sellers: hold the price. Leak listings: reprice toward the Etsy reference where the price is the likely brake (Zebra, Owl, Heart Disco Ball, Apple, Viking entry variant). Where the price is already at or below the reference (Golden Abstract), fix photos and copy instead. Tiers: entry $35-50 (just over the $35 free-shipping line), core $50-90, hero $90-200 (travertine, mirrors). Prefer occasional real sales (Cyber Specials) over permanently stacked coupons.

Shipping & returns. See the "Shipping and returns" section: free shipping guarantee, $0 additional items, real international cost, accurate processing, and a 14-30 day returns policy.
