3. Unit economics per sale (live prices)

Model: the buyer pays the item price plus $39 shipping (as in the listings). Etsy takes 6.5% transaction and 3% + $0.25 processing on the order total, plus a $0.20 renewal listing fee per sale. Offsite Ads are 15% of the order total, capped at $100, and only apply when an order comes from an offsite ad. The seller pays the supplier cost and a $39 label (a formula constant, not a carrier quote). Packaging and refinishing labour are $0 placeholders. The sale price is the list price × 0.65 (35% sale from Oct 12). Net % = net ÷ order total. Target: ≥ 30% net.
